Welcome to **KGISL-CAMPUS-SOLVERS**, an open-source engineering initiative anchored by the **Google Student Community at KGiSL Institute of Technology (KiTE), Autonomous, Coimbatore**.

Instead of writing disposable toy assignments, we build real-world, production-ready, open-source software that directly addresses the everyday academic, administrative, and student-life challenges faced across our departments:
* **Computer Science and Engineering (Cyber Security)**
* **Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)**
* **Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI & DS)**
* **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI & ML)**
* **Information Technology (IT)**
* **Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS)**
* **Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)**
* **Mechanical Engineering (Mech)**
