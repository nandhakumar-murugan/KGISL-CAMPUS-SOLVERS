# 🏛️ Campus Notice Copilot for WhatsApp (Chrome Extension)

> **Auto-extracts college circulars, exam dates, deadlines, and lab submissions from WhatsApp Web into an organized sidebar with 1-click Google Calendar sync.**

Part of the **KGISL-CAMPUS-SOLVERS** open-source initiative by the Google Student Community at KGiSL Institute of Technology (KiTE).

---

## ✨ Features
* 🚨 **Auto-Categorization**: Automatically classifies incoming messages into **Exams & Tests**, **Official Circulars**, and **Lab / Assignment Deadlines**.
* 📅 **1-Click Google Calendar Sync**: Add exam cutoffs, assignment due dates, and meetings directly to your Google Calendar.
* 📋 **Notice Drawer**: Sleek, non-intrusive floating drawer injected directly into WhatsApp Web.
* 📥 **Markdown Export**: Export your semester notice digest as a clean `.md` document.
* 🔒 **100% Client-Side Privacy**: All processing runs locally inside your browser sandbox. No messages are ever sent to external cloud servers.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in 30 Seconds (Developer Mode)

1. Open **Google Chrome** (or Edge / Brave).
2. Go to `chrome://extensions/` in your address bar.
3. Turn **ON** the toggle for **Developer mode** in the top right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top left).
5. Select this folder:
   ```text
   C:\Users\smnk2\Downloads\campus-notice-copilot-extension
   ```
6. Open **[web.whatsapp.com](https://web.whatsapp.com)**. You will see the floating pin icon (`📌`) on the bottom right corner. Click it to open your Notice Drawer!
