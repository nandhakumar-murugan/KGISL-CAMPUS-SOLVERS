# KGiSL Campus Solvers - Engineering Onboarding & FAQs

Welcome to KGiSL Campus Solvers! This guide provides answers to common questions for new student developers and contributors.

---

## 💡 Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How can I contribute to Campus Solvers projects?
- Explore our active sub-projects in projects/.
- Pick an open issue or suggest a utility tool for campus automation.
- Fork the repository, create a descriptive feature branch, and submit a Pull Request.

### 2. What tech stack are we using?
- **Python 3.10+**: Data processing, Streamlit web applications, and AI integrations.
- **FastAPI / REST**: Campus microservices and API endpoints.
- **Markdown & Git**: Documentation, student study packs, and collaborative problem-solving.

### 3. How do I report campus tech challenges?
- Open an Issue with the tag problem-statement.
- Provide clear context, who it impacts, and your suggested solution approach.
